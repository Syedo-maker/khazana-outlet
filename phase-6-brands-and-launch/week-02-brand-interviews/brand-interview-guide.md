# Week 2: Brand interview guide

Target: 15 to 20 interviews. Keep each one to 20 minutes unless they want to
keep talking. Ask, do not pitch. If you are speaking more than a third of the
time, the interview has failed.

## Before the call

- Check the brand website and Instagram for sale sections and outlet stores
- Note in the tracker whether they already have a clearance channel
- Have the tracker row open so you can fill it in live

## Ground rules

1. Ask what happened, not what they would do. Past behaviour is evidence,
   hypotheticals are noise.
2. Never ask "would you use a platform like this". Everyone says yes and it
   means nothing.
3. Numbers over adjectives. "A lot of stock" is useless, "about 4,000 pieces
   worth 6 million rupees at cost" is a finding.
4. Stay quiet after a question. Silence makes people add the important part.
5. Write down their exact words for objections. You will reuse the wording.

## The questions

### Part 1: the situation (5 minutes)

1. Walk me through what happens to a collection that does not sell out. Where
   does it physically go?
2. Roughly how much stock is sitting in your warehouse right now that is past
   its season? In pieces and in rupees at cost.
3. How much of a typical production run ends up unsold? What percentage is
   normal for you, and what percentage is a bad season?
4. Which categories or product types get stuck most often?

### Part 2: what they do today (6 minutes)

5. The last time you cleared a large quantity of old stock, what did you
   actually do? Who did you sell it to?
6. What did you get for it, as a percentage of the original retail price?
7. How quickly were you paid, and in what form?
8. Who decides this: you, finance, or the owner?
9. Have you ever had stock destroyed, donated or written off rather than
   sold? If yes, why was that the better option?

Question 6 is the single most important number in all of Phase 0. Your whole
price proposition is "better than what a dealer pays". You cannot build that
without the real figure from at least five brands.

### Part 3: the pain (4 minutes)

10. What goes wrong with the way you do it now?
11. Has your own stock ever turned up somewhere you did not want it, with
    your name on it? What happened?
12. If that stock turned into cash next month instead of next year, what
    would you do with the money?

Question 11 finds the brands who have been burned. They are your best first
customers because the pain is already real to them.

### Part 4: the test (5 minutes)

13. If there were a controlled channel where only verified business buyers
    could see your stock, and you approved every listing before it went
    live, what would worry you most about it?
14. What would the listing need to hide for you to be comfortable? Brand
    name, product photos, city, nothing at all?
15. What commission would make this obviously worth doing compared to what
    you do now?
16. Would you be willing to put one lot in as a test in [month]?

On question 15, let them give a number first. Do not anchor them with the 10
to 20 percent from the plan. If brands consistently say 5 percent, the whole
business model changes and you need to know that in week 2, not in month six.

## After every interview, within 24 hours

1. Fill in these tracker fields: `dead_stock_estimate_pkr`,
   `current_disposal_method`, `price_received_percent`, `payment_speed_days`,
   `main_objection`, `commission_acceptable_percent`, `test_commitment`.
2. Write up the interview using `brand-interview-notes-template.md`, one file
   per brand, named `notes-<brand-name>.md` in this folder.
3. Send the thank you and commitment ask, template 7.

## What a successful week 2 looks like

- 15 or more interviews done and written up
- Five or more real figures for question 6, so you know the dealer price
- A ranked list of objections by how often they came up
- Five or more written test commitments
- A clear answer to this question: do brands have enough pain to change
  behaviour, or is the dealer channel already good enough?

Be willing to hear no. If 15 brands tell you the dealer channel works fine
and they are paid cash in a day, that is a real finding and the model has to
change. Record it honestly instead of hunting for the five brands who agree
with you.
