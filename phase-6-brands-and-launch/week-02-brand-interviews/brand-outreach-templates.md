# Week 2: Brand outreach templates

Note on language: these templates are written in English because the project
rules require English in the repository. On a phone call or a WhatsApp chat
with a Pakistani brand owner you will naturally speak Urdu. Translate as you
speak, the structure is what matters. If you want ready made Roman Urdu
versions kept in the repo, say so and they will be added as a separate file.

## Rules for every first contact

1. Never use the words "dead stock" to a brand in the first message. They
   hear "your product failed". Say leftover stock, previous season stock, or
   surplus inventory.
2. Do not pitch a platform. You do not have one. Ask for 15 minutes of their
   knowledge. People give time to someone learning, not to someone selling.
3. Never promise a price, a timeline or a buyer in the first contact.
4. Name the brand image protection early. It is their first objection and
   raising it yourself buys trust.
5. One ask per message. The ask is always a short call or a visit.

## Template 1: LinkedIn connection note (300 character limit)

> Hello [Name], I am researching how Pakistani brands handle previous season
> and surplus inventory, and I am speaking to supply chain managers this
> month. Would you be open to a 15 minute call? I am not selling anything, I
> am trying to understand the problem properly.

## Template 2: LinkedIn or email message after they connect

> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for connecting. I am doing research on what happens to previous
> season and surplus stock at Pakistani brands, and [Brand] came up as one of
> the companies that manages this well.
>
> I am building a plan for a proper, controlled channel where brands can
> clear surplus inventory without the stock appearing in the wrong market
> under their name. Before building anything I want to understand how this
> actually works today, from people who deal with it.
>
> Could I have 15 minutes this week or next? I will send the questions in
> advance so it is quick, and I am happy to share what I learn across the
> industry.
>
> Regards,
> Muhammad Ibrahim

## Template 3: WhatsApp first message (short, for small brands)

> Hello, this is Muhammad Ibrahim. I am doing research on previous season
> leftover stock with Pakistani brands. May I ask you two or three questions
> about how [Brand] handles it, either on a call or here in chat? I am not
> selling anything.

## Template 4: Cold call opening (landline, head office)

Ask the operator:

> Good morning. Could you connect me to whoever handles inventory planning or
> supply chain, please? I am calling about surplus stock, not a sale.

When connected:

> Hello, my name is Muhammad Ibrahim. I am doing research on how Pakistani
> brands clear previous season and surplus stock, and I am speaking to people
> who deal with inventory. Do you have three minutes now, or may I call back
> at a better time?

If they say three minutes, ask only questions 1, 2 and 7 from the interview
guide, then ask for a longer call.

## Template 5: Visit request for a local brand

> Hello [Name], I am researching surplus inventory in Pakistani brands. I am
> in [City] on [day] and would value 20 minutes at your office or warehouse.
> Seeing the stock in person would help me understand far more than a call.
> Would [day and time] suit you?

A warehouse visit is worth five phone calls. Ask for it with any brand in
your city.

## Template 6: Follow up after no reply

Send once, after four working days. Never more than twice.

> Hello [Name], following up on my note about surplus stock research. If this
> is not for you, no problem at all, just let me know and I will stop
> following up. If someone else at [Brand] is the right person, I would be
> grateful for the name.

The second sentence matters. Giving people a clean way to say no raises reply
rates and it is honest.

## Template 7: Thank you and commitment ask (after the interview)

Send within 24 hours of every interview.

> Hello [Name], thank you for your time today. The three things I took away
> were [point 1], [point 2] and [point 3]. Please correct me if I have any of
> that wrong.
>
> One ask. When we run the first controlled sale in [month], with [the brand
> image protection they asked for], would you be willing to put one lot in as
> a test? No commitment beyond that single lot, and nothing is listed without
> your written approval.

That last paragraph is the whole point of week 2. The exit criterion is 10
brands saying yes in writing, so every interview must end with this ask.

## Outreach volume targets for the week

| Activity | Target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First contacts sent | 50 | Across LinkedIn, WhatsApp, calls, visits |
| Replies | 20 to 25 | Expect roughly half, lower for Tier B |
| Interviews completed | 15 to 20 | The real target for the week |
| Warehouse visits | 3 or more | Highest information value |
| Written test commitments | 5 or more | Builds toward the 10 needed |

Log every single contact in `../trackers/brand-pipeline.csv` on the same day.
A contact you do not log is a contact you will repeat by accident.
