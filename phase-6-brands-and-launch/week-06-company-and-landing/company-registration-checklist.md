# Week 6: Company registration and compliance checklist

Goal for the week: a registered company, an NTN, a business bank account, and
a live landing page collecting the waiting list.

Important: this is a practical checklist, not legal or tax advice. Confirm
every item with a corporate consultant or a tax practitioner before you file.
Fees and procedures change, so verify current amounts on the official portals
rather than trusting any figure written here or on a blog.

## Part 1: company registration with SECP

### What is confirmed about the process

- Registration runs through the SECP online portal. SECP has moved to an
  online corporate registry, and applicants can complete submission, payment
  and certificate issuance digitally without visiting an office.
- SECP has introduced a single online procedure intended to complete company
  registration within a few working hours once documents and name clearance
  are in order. In practice, allow two to seven working days.
- A private limited company requires a minimum of two directors and two
  shareholders, who may be the same two people.
- Company name reservation carries a small separate fee.
- The electronic filing fee for registration is lower than physical filing,
  and the total fee scales with the nominal share capital. For a company with
  nominal share capital not exceeding 100,000 rupees the electronic
  submission fee is small, with an additional amount per 100,000 rupees of
  capital above that.

Verify the current fee schedule on the SECP portal at the time of filing.

### Decisions to make before you file

| Decision | Options | Your choice | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Structure | Sole proprietorship, AOP, single member company, private limited | | Private limited is what the plan assumes, and it is what brands and investors expect to contract with |
| Company name | Three choices in order of preference | | SECP may reject a name, so always submit alternatives |
| Nominal share capital | | | Affects the filing fee, keep it modest at the start |
| Directors | Minimum two for a private limited | | |
| Shareholding split | | | Agree this in writing with any co founder before filing, not after |
| Registered office address | | | Must be a real address that can receive legal notices |
| Principal line of business | | | Describe it broadly enough to cover marketplace, e commerce and software services |

The shareholding row is the one that destroys companies. Agree percentages,
vesting and what happens if someone leaves, in writing, before registration.

## Part 2: tax registration with FBR

- [ ] Register for an NTN on the FBR IRIS portal
- [ ] Decide with a tax consultant whether sales tax registration is required
      now or when turnover reaches a threshold. A marketplace that collects
      payment on behalf of sellers has specific sales tax and withholding
      implications, so get this checked before the first real transaction.
- [ ] Understand withholding obligations on payments to brands and couriers
- [ ] Set up a bookkeeping method from day one, even a spreadsheet, so that
      commission income and payouts are separable at year end

## Part 3: business bank account

- [ ] Open a current account in the company name once incorporated
- [ ] Ask the bank specifically about receiving collections on behalf of third
      parties, because that is what escrow is, and some banks treat it
      differently
- [ ] Set up online banking and a second signatory
- [ ] Keep company money and personal money strictly separate from day one

## Part 4: payment and e commerce compliance

- [ ] Apply for a JazzCash merchant account, note the documents required
- [ ] Apply for an Easypaisa merchant account
- [ ] Get quotes from a card gateway such as Safepay or PayFast
- [ ] Ask each provider about settlement time, because the gap between buyer
      payment and brand payout is your working capital problem
- [ ] Confirm with the gateway whether your model counts as a marketplace,
      since marketplace onboarding requirements differ from a single merchant
- [ ] Note any State Bank of Pakistan or PTA requirements your provider
      mentions and record them here

## Part 5: documents to have before the first real sale

These are needed for week 7, not later.

- [ ] Seller agreement with brands, covering commission, payment terms,
      returns, brand image protection options, and who owns the stock while
      it is in transit. Have a lawyer draft or review this one, it is the
      contract the business runs on.
- [ ] Buyer terms and conditions
- [ ] Privacy policy, which you need before the landing page collects phone
      numbers
- [ ] Return and dispute policy
- [ ] Invoice template with company details and NTN
