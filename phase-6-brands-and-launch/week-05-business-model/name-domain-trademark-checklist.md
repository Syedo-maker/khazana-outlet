# Week 5: Name, domain and trademark checklist

The name is Khazana Outlet. This week locks it in, or finds out early that it
cannot be locked and changes it before anything is printed, registered or
built. Changing a name after Phase 1 is expensive, changing it this week
costs nothing.

## Step 3: trademark registration at IPO Pakistan

What is confirmed about the process as of the latest available information:

- Multi class applications are not possible in Pakistan. A separate
  application is required for each class.
- A trademark registration is valid for 10 years from the date of
  application, renewable for further 10 year terms.
- Filing requires the prescribed application forms, clear reproductions of
  the mark, a list of goods or services, and a Power of Attorney if you file
  through an agent.
- IPO Pakistan searches its records for similar marks in the same or similar
  class, examines the application, may raise objections, and publishes
  accepted marks in the Trade Marks Journal.
- Official fee amounts and current form numbers change, so confirm them on
  the IPO Pakistan website or with a trademark agent before filing. Treat any
  figure you read on a third party blog as unreliable.

Classes likely relevant to a marketplace. Confirm with an agent, since the
correct classes depend on what you actually sell versus what service you
provide:

| Class | Covers | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 35 | Retail and wholesale services, bringing goods together for others, online marketplace services, advertising | Almost certainly yes, this is the core marketplace class |
| 25 | Clothing, footwear, headgear | Yes if you ever sell own label or private label goods |
| 16 | Paper goods and stationery | Only if stationery becomes a core own label category |
| 9 | Downloadable software and mobile applications | Worth considering for the app |
| 42 | Software as a service, platform development | Worth considering alongside 35 |

application or a badly drafted specification of services.

## Step 4: the brand image question the name raises

The plan already notes that brands dislike the word "dead". Khazana Outlet
avoids that, which is good. Two further things to decide this week:

1. Do brands see the same public name you show buyers? If a brand is worried
   about being associated with an outlet platform, a neutral corporate name
   for brand facing contracts and a consumer name for the storefront is a
   legitimate structure. Decide now whether you need both.
2. The word outlet signals discount, which helps with buyers and may hurt
   with premium brands. Test the name on at least five brands in week 2 or
   week 5 by simply asking: if your stock were sold on a site called Khazana
   Outlet, would that bother you? Record the answers.

If three or more of five brands react badly, consider keeping Khazana Outlet
as the B2C storefront for year 2 and using a neutral B2B name for the brand
facing platform. That is a cheap decision now and an expensive one later.
