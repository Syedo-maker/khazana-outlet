# Phase 6: Brands and launch

## Purpose

Put real brands and real buyers on a platform that already works. This is the
last phase in [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md), and it starts at roughly week 49,
after the platform has been built, hardened, documented and released.

The week numbers inside this folder are week 1 to week 8 of this phase, which
is week 49 onward of the project.

Note on the order: this material was originally written as Phase 0, to run
before any code. The plan was changed deliberately to build the platform
first. The work itself does not change, but one thing gets much easier. You
are no longer asking a brand to imagine a platform. You open a laptop, show a
working system with their name hidden, a real manifest, a real approval gate
and a real audit trail, and ask for one lot.

The warning that comes with that order: until this phase runs, every
assumption about commission rates, discount bands and buyer behaviour is
still an assumption. That is why the platform keeps all of it as
configuration rather than code.

## What is in this folder

Every week has a folder with the documents, scripts and templates needed to

## Important: what this folder can and cannot do

These documents are the instrument. You are the one who has to use it.

Work only you can do:
- Call, message and visit brand owners and supply chain managers
- Call and visit resellers, shopkeepers and online sellers
- Register the company with SECP and get the NTN
- Sell real stock by hand in week 7
- Make the go or no go decision in week 8

Work the documents already do for you:
- Who to contact, in what order, and what to say
- Exactly which questions to ask and how to record the answers
- How to score the categories instead of guessing
- How to model commission and unit economics
- The registration and trademark checklists
- The landing page that collects the waiting list

Nothing in here invents market data. Every number that needs a real source
is marked `[VERIFY]`. Fill those in with what you actually hear and measure.

## Exit criteria (all four must be true before the public launch)

1. At least 10 brands have said yes in writing, meaning a signed or emailed
   agreement in principle to list stock, not a polite maybe on a call.
2. At least 50 resellers are on the waiting list with a phone number and a
   city, collected through the landing page or by hand.
3. At least 10 lots have sold, with money received and delivery completed.
   Sell the first few by hand over WhatsApp even though the platform exists,
   because doing it manually once shows you what the platform gets wrong.
4. Unit economics show a positive contribution margin per order at the
   commission rate the brands actually accepted, not the rate you hoped for.

If criterion 3 fails, do not launch publicly. The original plan put it
plainly, and it holds just as well with a platform in hand: if 10 to 20 lots
will not sell, a nicer website will not sell them either.

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday: pick the week's folder, read its document, plan the week's calls
- Daily: log every contact in the matching tracker CSV on the same day
- Friday: write the week's output file, update this README's progress table
- Friday: if a week's target was missed, decide to extend or to cut scope,
  and write that decision down in `week-08-mvp-scope/go-no-go-review.md`
