## 3. How to read the result honestly

The uncomfortable cases are the valuable ones. Find yours.

| What happened | What it probably means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brands keen, resellers keen, lots sold fast | The thesis holds | Start Phase 1 as planned |
| Brands keen, lots did not sell | Supply is not the problem, demand is. You have a buyer acquisition business, not a supply one | Spend four more weeks on demand before building. A platform will not create buyers |
| Resellers keen, brands would not commit | The dealer channel is good enough for brands | Consider buying lots outright and reselling, which is the Phase 3 liquidation model brought forward. Different business, needs capital |
| Both keen but margin was negative | Pricing or lot size is wrong | Raise minimum lot value, renegotiate commission, cut a cost. Do not build and hope for scale |
| Everything worked but only because you did it all yourself | Normal at this stage | Identify the manual step that cost the most hours, build that first |
| Nothing worked | The idea as framed does not fit this market | Stop. Write down what you learned. That is a real outcome, not a waste |

The third row is the one to watch. The plan is built on brands participating.
If they will not, nothing in Phase 1 fixes it, and spending 20 weeks building
M1 to M8 would be the most expensive way possible to learn that.

## 5. Phase 1 readiness checklist

Do not start Phase 1 until all of these are true.

- [ ] Company registered, NTN issued, bank account open
- [ ] Seller agreement reviewed by a lawyer and signed with at least 3 brands
- [ ] Starting categories decided and written down
- [ ] Starting city decided
- [ ] Commission structure agreed with at least 5 brands
- [ ] Unit economics model updated with real week 7 numbers
- [ ] MVP requirements complete, with the out of scope list agreed
- [ ] Developers hired or contracted, with a start date
- [ ] Budget confirmed for at least the 20 weeks of Phase 1
- [ ] Repository, environments and documentation structure set up, which is
      Phase 1 week 11 work and can start early
- [ ] Waiting list of 50 or more resellers ready to invite to the beta
