# Week 1: Brand target list

## How to use this list

The brands below are publicly known Pakistani brands, grouped into tiers by
how likely they are to say yes early. Every name still needs verification:
confirm the company is active, find the right person, and check whether they
already have an outlet or clearance channel.

No contact names, emails or phone numbers are filled in here on purpose.
Inventing a contact wastes a week of your time when the call goes nowhere.
Find each contact yourself using the sourcing method below, then record it in
`../trackers/brand-pipeline.csv`.

Target for the week: 50 verified brands in the tracker, with a named contact
or a clear route to one for at least 30 of them.

## Who you are actually looking for

Not the CEO, and not the shop manager. The person who feels the pain of dead
stock is one of these, in this order:

1. Supply chain manager or inventory or merchandise planner
2. Head of retail operations
3. Finance manager or CFO, because dead stock is trapped cash on their books
4. Owner or managing director, only for small brands where he is all of these

## How to find the contact (in order of hit rate)

1. LinkedIn search: company name plus one of the job titles above. Note the
   person's name and title in the tracker, then send a connection request
   with a one line note.
2. Brand's own website: careers page, contact page, head office number. Call
   the landline and ask for the supply chain or inventory department by name.
3. Instagram or Facebook business page: ask for the head office email, not a
   sale. Do not pitch in the first DM.
4. Warm introductions: list every person you know in retail, textiles or
   logistics, and ask each one for two introductions.
5. Trade bodies and events: PRGMEA, APTMA, Pakistan Retail Business Council,
   and textile or fashion expos. Membership directories are a contact source.

## Tier A: small and medium brands (start here, weeks 2 and 3)

These have real dead stock, no organised clearance channel, and a decision
maker you can actually reach. The plan says start with small and medium
brands, so these get your first 20 calls.

Fill this tier yourself, because small brands are local and change fast. Good
hunting grounds:

- Instagram and Facebook clothing and footwear brands with 10k to 300k
  followers that run their own production, not resellers
- Brands with 1 to 5 physical outlets in Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad,
  Sialkot, Gujranwala or Multan
- Daraz sellers with a registered brand name and their own manufacturing
- Exporters with a domestic side brand, the export rejects and overruns are
  exactly the stock you want

Record at least 20 Tier A brands. This is the tier most likely to convert.

## Tier C: stock lot dealers and exporters (supply, not partnership)

These already trade leftover stock. They are not the brands in your pitch,
but they can fill the catalogue while you are still signing brands, and they
tell you the real market prices faster than any brand will.

- Export surplus dealers in Karachi: Zainab Market, Rex Centre in Saddar
- Lahore wholesale: Azam Cloth Market, Anarkali, Shah Alam Market
- Faisalabad: textile overrun and reject dealers
- Online: OLX Pakistan stock lot listings, TradeKey and TradeWheel Pakistani
  stock lot suppliers

Caution: a lot of this supply is export rejects and low quality leftovers,
not original brand dead stock. If you fill the catalogue with it, you become
Zainab Market with a website and you lose the one thing brands will pay for,
which is a channel that does not damage their name. Use Tier C to learn
prices and to test demand, label it honestly, and keep it separate from
branded listings.

## Fields to record for every brand

Use `../trackers/brand-pipeline.csv`. The columns are:

`brand_name, category, tier, size_estimate, outlets, has_existing_clearance,
city, contact_name, contact_title, contact_route, contact_detail, status,
first_contact_date, last_contact_date, dead_stock_estimate_pkr,
current_disposal_method, main_objection, next_step, notes`

Two columns decide everything and most people skip them:

- `has_existing_clearance`: if the brand already runs outlet stores or a
  permanent sale section, your pitch has to beat what they already do.
- `current_disposal_method`: where the stock goes today. The answer here is
  your real competitor, and it is usually a named wholesale dealer, not a
  platform.
