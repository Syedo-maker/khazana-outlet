Two sides, one channel

Right now surplus stock usually leaves through one dealer, for cash, with no record and no control over where it ends up. That is fast, and it is why it happens. It is also why brands lose both margin and control.

If you are a brand

  • Sell whole lots to many competing buyers instead of one dealer
  • Approve every listing, with your name shown or hidden
  • Block cities where you have your own stores
  • Get a documented, invoiced sale for your books, not a cash deal
  • See what sold, where, and at what price

If you are a reseller or shop

  • Original branded stock, not export rejects, clearly labelled
  • An honest manifest: piece count, sizes, colours and condition
  • Your money held until you confirm the goods arrived as described
  • Lot sizes a small shop can actually afford
  • Delivery to your city through a tracked courier

How a sale works

  1. The brand lists a lotPiece count, sizes, condition and photographs, with the protection level the brand chooses.
  2. We check itEvery listing is reviewed before it is visible. No listing goes live without the brand's written approval.
  3. Verified buyers orderOnly resellers who have passed verification can see protected listings.
  4. The money is heldPayment stays with the platform until delivery is confirmed by the buyer.
  5. The brand is paidFunds are released to the brand after confirmation, minus our commission. Nothing else is deducted.

Questions brands ask first

Will my discounted stock damage my full price sales?

That is the risk we are built around. Use a private sale so only verified business buyers see the lot, hide your brand name on the listing, and lock the sale out of the cities where you have stores. You set this per lot, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

How is this better than selling to a dealer?

A dealer gives you one price, take it or leave it. Here several verified buyers see the same lot, so the price is set by demand. You also get a proper invoice and a record of the sale, which a cash deal in a wholesale market cannot give you.

When do I get paid?

After the buyer confirms delivery, the payment is released to you minus our commission. During the first sales we will agree the exact timetable with you in writing before you list anything.

What does it cost?

A commission on completed sales only. Listing is free during the first sales. We are still agreeing the rate with the brands joining now, which is one reason to talk to us early.

I am a reseller. How do I know the goods are real and the count is right?

Every lot carries a manifest with piece count, size breakdown, colours and condition. Your payment is held until you confirm what arrived matches it. If it does not, you are not left arguing on WhatsApp with a stranger.

Are you selling export rejects?

No. Our listings are brand surplus and previous season stock. If a lot is factory seconds or has defects, it is graded and labelled as such, so you always know what you are buying.
