# Guide for brands

For brands selling surplus stock on Khazana Outlet: how to list a lot, what
the AI does and does not do for you, and how you are paid. Your brand's own
data is only ever visible to your own staff and the Khazana team.

## Getting started

During the beta, Khazana is invite only: the team sends you a sign in link
on WhatsApp. Open it, accept the beta terms, and you are in. Each link works
once; ask the team for a new one when you need to sign in again. Then register
your brand from the dashboard. The Khazana team verifies it from your
registration documents, which you upload under your brand's settings. Until
the brand is verified, lots cannot go live.

Your **policy** is set with the Khazana team and can be changed at any time:
commission, discount band, minimum lot value, whether part lots and cash on
delivery are allowed, payout terms, the cities a lot may be kept out of, and
whether bargaining and automatic markdowns are allowed at all.

## Listing a lot

1. **Lots**, then create a lot: title, category, season, condition grade,
   retail price per piece and the lot price.
2. **Photographs.** Upload front, back, label and detail shots. The platform
   suggests a title, description and grade from them, as a draft you edit,
   never as the listing itself.
3. **Brand protection.** If your marks must not appear, choose how the lot is
   shown: under your name, privately to verified resellers, or unbranded. On
   an unbranded lot, buyers see a label instead of your name, and the platform
   finds logos in your photographs and blurs them; you review every blurred
   photograph before anyone sees it.
4. **Manifest.** Enter sizes, colours and piece counts, or **Read from a
   spreadsheet**: upload your stock file and the platform reads it into rows
   for you to check. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.
5. **Price.** The pricing suggestion proposes a lot price inside your discount
   band, with its reasons and comparable lots. You decide the price.
6. Submit. The listing goes into **Approvals**, where you, and where your
   policy requires it the Khazana team, approve it. A risk score helps the
   reviewer; it never approves or rejects anything itself.

## Selling during the beta: cash on delivery, paid to you

In the beta your buyers pay **you**, in cash, through your own courier.
Khazana never holds the money. The order page says "Paid to the brand on
delivery".

1. **An order arrives.** Pack it. The order shows the total to collect: the
   goods plus delivery.
2. **Send it with your usual courier** (TCS, Leopards, M&P, Trax or any other),
   booked to collect that amount on delivery.
3. **On the order page, enter the courier's name and the tracking number** from
   the slip, then **Mark as sent**.
4. **When the courier reports back,** press **Delivered, cash collected**, or
   **Refused at the door**. A refused order is cancelled and its pieces go back
   on sale.
5. The buyer has a few days to confirm the goods or report a problem. On
   confirmation, the order shows the **commission you owe**: your agreed rate
   on the goods the buyer kept.
6. **On the 1st of each month**, **Commission** lists an invoice for the month
   before, which you can print or save as a PDF. Pay it by bank transfer as
   agreed with the Khazana team, quoting the invoice number, and send them the
   reference. The page shows when it is marked paid.

If a buyer reports a problem, the Khazana team looks at both sides and records
any refund you agree to give the buyer directly. Commission is then owed only
on what the buyer kept, and nothing on a full refund.

## Selling through the platform

When payments through Khazana are switched on for your brand, it works like
this instead:

- **Orders** shows every order on your lots. The buyer's address appears once
  the order is paid. Book the courier from the order when the goods are
  packed; the label is generated for you, and tracking updates the order.
- Money is held by Khazana from the buyer's payment until they confirm the
  goods arrived, or their dispute window ends.
- **Payouts** shows what you are owed, when it is scheduled under your payout
  terms, and what has been paid, with the bank reference.

When the day's AI budget has been used, the drafts, suggestions and logo
checks pause until tomorrow and each page says so. Everything still works by
hand: you write the listing, type the manifest or import the spreadsheet,
price inside your own band, and mark logos yourself.

## Agents that work inside your limits

Two features act on a lot by themselves, and only if you turn them on, per
lot or for your whole brand. Both work only inside limits you set, and every
change they make is in the lot's history:

- **Bargaining.** Buyers can make offers, and the assistant counters on your
  behalf, never below the floor your policy sets and never more than one step
  at a time. You can switch it off at any time, which closes open
  negotiations.
- **Automatic markdowns.** A lot that is not selling is marked down a step at
  a time, at most as often and as far as your policy allows, and never past
  your floor or your discount band.

## Understanding your stock

- **Dashboard**: what is live, selling, waiting and paid.
- **Ask about your stock**: ask in plain words what is slow, what sold where,
  or how much room there is to discount a lot. It answers from your own data
  only and never changes anything.
- **Forecast**: how long each lot is likely to take to sell, and warnings when
  a product line is building up unsold stock.
- **Reports**: sales by city, category and period.
- **Size chart**: your sizes mapped to the standard chart buyers see.

Stock that does not sell can be offered to upcycling or donation partners
from the lot page, with the Khazana team's approval.
