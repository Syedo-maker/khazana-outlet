# Guide for resellers

For shop owners and resellers buying surplus stock on Khazana Outlet. The app
works on any phone browser, in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu: choose under
**Language** at the top of every page.

## Signing in

During the beta, Khazana is invite only. The Khazana team sends you a link
on WhatsApp. Open it, check it shows your name and number, enter your shop's
name and city, accept the beta terms, and you are signed in. There is no
password. Each link works once and expires after a few days; ask the team for
a new one if yours has. If you sign out, you are signed out on every device.

The team checks your business before you can order; until then you can
browse.

Once SMS codes are switched on, the login page asks for your mobile number,
11 digits starting with 03, and sends a six digit code instead.

## Before you buy: your business profile

Open **Business profile** and fill in your shop's name, city and address.
Some brands sell only to verified resellers, and some lots are private until
you are verified. Verification is done by the Khazana team; the page shows
where yours stands.

Your city matters: a brand can keep a lot out of particular cities, and
delivery is priced from the brand's city to yours. You can still buy for a
shop in another city by choosing that city at checkout.

## Finding stock

- **Catalogue** lists every lot you may buy. Each card shows the condition
  grade, the discount off retail and the price.
- Search by words, filter by category, city, grade, season, price, discount
  and number of pieces, and sort. Save a search to come back to it.
- Search by photograph: upload a picture of what you sell and the catalogue
  shows the closest lots.
- Recommendations at the top of the catalogue are chosen from what you have
  bought and searched for, and each says why it was chosen.

Open a lot to see its photographs, the full manifest of sizes and colours,
any defects the brand declared, and the price per piece. The profit helper
works out what you would make at your own selling price, with every
assumption listed.

## Buying

1. On a lot, choose the whole lot or, where the brand allows it, the sizes
   and colours you want, then **Add to cart**.
2. In the **Cart**, enter the delivery city and full address. During the
   beta, brands are paid **cash on delivery**: you pay the brand's courier
   when the goods arrive, and nothing is paid online. The cart says so.
3. **Place order.** The stock is reserved for you from this moment.
4. The brand sends it with its courier and the order page shows the courier
   and tracking number. Pay the courier the total shown when it arrives. You
   may refuse a delivery at the door; the order is then cancelled and you owe
   nothing.

Where a brand is paid through Khazana instead, you choose JazzCash,
Easypaisa, bank transfer or cash on delivery at checkout, and your money is
held by Khazana until you confirm the goods arrived.

## Bargaining

Where a brand allows it, a lot shows a bargaining option. Make an offer and
the brand's assistant answers with a counter offer, within the limits the
brand set. An agreed price is held for you for 48 hours: place the order at
that price within the time. You can still buy at the listed price instead.

## Buying together

**Buy together** lets three or four shops in the same city share one lot that
is too big for one of them. One shop opens a pool and says what it needs; the
others join with their own sizes and colours. When the pool covers the lot,
the organiser places it, and each shop gets its own order and pays its own
share. If stock is short for a size, it is shared out in proportion to what
each asked for.

**Build a mixed lot** puts together a lot from several brands within your
budget and piece count, priced exactly as checkout would price it.

## After you order

The order page shows every step: paid, being prepared, shipped with tracking,
delivered. When the goods arrive, check them:

- If everything is right, press **Goods received correctly**.
- If something is wrong, report a problem before the date the page shows,
  with what is wrong. The Khazana team decides disputes, and may refund in
  full or in part.

If you do nothing, the order is confirmed automatically when the window ends.

## Selling it on: the resell kit

For a paid order, the resell kit makes captions in English, Roman Urdu and
Urdu, a social media post, a WhatsApp catalogue, price tags at your selling
price, and square photographs ready to post. Where the brand asked for its
name to stay hidden, it stays hidden in the kit too.

## The assistant

**Ask the assistant** answers questions about the catalogue in English, Roman
Urdu or Urdu: what is available, what suits your shop, what an order is
doing. It answers from the catalogue's real data, and you can ask it up to 40
questions a day. The same assistant answers on WhatsApp from your registered
number, including voice notes up to two minutes.

The assistant suggests; it never changes an order or a price.
